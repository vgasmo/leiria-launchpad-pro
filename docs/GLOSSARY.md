# Project Glossary & Terminology

To ensure consistency across English (EN) and Portuguese (PT) locales.

| Concept | English (EN) | Portuguese (PT-PT) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Workspace** | Workspace | Workspace | Do not translate to "Espaço de Trabalho" unless referring to physical office. |
| **Lead** | Lead | Lead | Potential startup/founder in CRM. |
| **Stage** | Stage | Fase | Pipeline or program stage. |
| **Action** | Action / Action Item | Ação / Item de Ação | Task assigned to a user. |
| **Task** | Task | Tarefa | Unit of work. |
| **Milestone** | Milestone | Marco | Significant goal/event. |
| **Invoice** | Invoice | Fatura | Billing document. |
| **Contract** | Contract | Contrato | Legal agreement. |
| **Office Space** | Office Space | Espaço de Escritório | Physical room/desk. |
| **Check-in** | Check-in | Check-in | Regular progress update. |
| **Health Score** | Health Score | Pontuação de Saúde / Health Score | Keep "Health Score" or "Saúde". |
| **Playbook** | Playbook | Playbook | Guided set of actions. |
| **Pitch Deck** | Pitch Deck | Pitch Deck | Presentation for investors. |
| **Consultant** | Consultant | Consultor | Staff member guiding startups. |
| **Founder** | Founder | Fundador | Startup creator. |
| **Mentor** | Mentor | Mentor | External expert. |

## Translation Rules

1. **Keep Industry Terms**: Words like "Workspace", "Lead", "Check-in", "Pitch Deck", "Playbook" are often used in English even in PT contexts in this ecosystem.
2. **PT-PT**: Use European Portuguese spelling (e.g., "Fatura" not "Boleto", "Equipa" not "Equipe", "Ação" not "Ação").
3. **Formal/Informal**: Use a professional but modern tone. Avoid overly formal "Vossa Excelência", treat the user as "o utilizador" or direct address "Crie a sua startup" (imperative/you).
